Case 1 · The first impression

The mystery roommate

You are interviewing three people who want to share a flat. One candidate is described like this:

“She is extremely organised, always checks the details twice, keeps her promises and notices immediately when something unusual happens. She is not especially talkative, but when she gives advice, she clearly knows what she is talking about.”

Which zodiac sign would you tentatively associate with this personality?

Case 2 · The group chat

Something has gone wrong

A group of friends is planning a birthday trip. At 11 p.m., the hotel cancels their booking. Read the messages and decide what each person is like.

Alex: “Fine. Let's not panic. I'll call three other hotels tomorrow and work out the most rational option.”

Sam: “WHY does this always happen to me?! Nobody ever does anything properly.”

Chris: “Oh well. We'll find somewhere. And if we don't, we can have the party at home.”

Jamie: “Honestly, I don't really care where we go. I just want everyone to listen to me.”

Case 3 · The astrology dating show

Red flag or unfair stereotype?

A contestant is described by the host. Decide which traits you can actually hear in the description.

“He never says what he really thinks. In front of powerful people, he is charming and supportive; five minutes later, he complains about them to everyone else. He also refuses to spend money, even when his friends are struggling.”

Which traits can you hear? Choose one or more.

Case 4 · The zodiac crisis

What happens when the situation changes?

A normally well-balanced, good-tempered person receives terrible news. Their reaction is completely unexpected.

They discover that their best friend has betrayed them. They are absolutely devastated. They stop answering messages, become unusually anti-social and spend the whole weekend alone. On Monday, however, they calmly explain what happened and decide what to do next.

Choose up to three words that could describe different parts of this situation.

Final case · Who is the sign?

The anonymous zodiac profile

“This person is strong-willed and courageous. They are open-minded enough to listen to ideas they disagree with, but they are not easily persuaded. They are knowledgeable about almost everything they become interested in. At work they are conscientious, alert and trustworthy. They are modest about their achievements, although other people sometimes think they are gifted. They are also surprisingly chatty once they feel comfortable. Their one problem: when someone is frustrating them, they can become very bad-tempered.”

Choose a sign and prepare a 30-second defence. Use at least 6 target words.
